# What would make ARCOLOGY more fun

v1 is a spreadsheet that plays itself: the autoplay operator survives the
century on 200 of 200 seeds, so there is no real decision to make. Fun comes
from decisions with tension, and the century is the asset. In order:

1. **Eras, not 5,200 identical weeks.** Divide the century into generations
   with their own pressures: the first decade is construction debt and
   skeptical neighbors; the 2040s are the climate (the Hudson rises, the
   summers lengthen); the 2060s the tower is old and its first residents are
   dying in it; the 2090s the city around it has changed. Each era changes the
   numbers and the events, so the strategy that won the last twenty years
   stops working.

2. **Decisions instead of levers.** Replace rent-plus-or-minus with occasional
   forced choices: events with two or three answers and consequences that
   last. The city offers to buy the transit spur; a union wants a contract; a
   developer wants the top forty floors; a storm leaves a thousand people in
   the lobby and you choose who gets beds. The current events only hit you.

3. **People.** A handful of named residents and staff who show up in the log,
   age across the century, complain, leave, come back, die. The chief engineer
   who built the fusion plant retires in year 31 and wear rates change.

4. **A reason the autoplay can lose.** Make crews the choke point, with triage
   every year between growth and maintenance. You cannot refit everything and
   build ahead at once.

5. **The building.** A cross-section of the tower on one canvas: decks stack
   up as you build, floors go dark as they wear, lights at night. Numbers
   become a place.

6. **An ending worth reaching.** Year 100 produces a report: peak population,
   how many people were born there, the worst winter, what the tower is called
   now, a grade. Then a shareable seed and score, the daily-challenge move.

The next prompt, per the vibe-game skill: eras plus decision events, the
operator extended to answer them, then a few hundred simulated centuries to
find where it still never loses. That is the "make it a LOT more fun" pass.
The drawing and the people come after, once something is at stake. Keep v1
playable at `v1/` when v2 lands.
